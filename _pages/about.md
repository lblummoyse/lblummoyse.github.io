I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Integrative Biophysics Group](https://www.exc.uni-konstanz.de/collective-behaviour/about-us/people/integrative-biophysics-group/) at the Centre for the Advanced Study of Collective Behaviour, University of Konstanz, working with Ahmed El Hady. I build mathematical and computational models to understand cognitive dynamics, from neuronal populations to foraging groups.

I trained in physics and biology at the École Normale Supérieure de Lyon, and received my PhD in computational neuroscience from INSA Lyon in 2023, supervised by Hugues Berry.

Research interests
======

## Cognitive processes underlying foraging

Animals constantly decide when to leave a food patch and where to go next, often while sharing information with others. I develop analytically tractable models of these decisions, based on evidence accumulation (drift-diffusion) processes, to link individual cognition to collective dynamics:

* **Social foraging in egalitarian groups**: how different forms of social information shape patch-leaving decisions, group cohesion and accuracy ([PRX Life, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1103/mqxr-1nyv)).
* **Social hierarchy**: how leaders and followers, and misinformation between them, shape foraging decisions ([Phys. Rev. Research, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1103/5vfb-jg8f)).
* **Models and data**: model selection and parameter identifiability for socio-cognitive foraging models ([arXiv, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36231)), and combining reinforcement-learning and drift-diffusion models to analyse human collective foraging ([PsyArXiv, 2025](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/a3ptk_v1)).

## Computational neuroscience

During my PhD, I developed theoretical models at increasing levels of abstraction to address questions arising from neuroscience experiments:

* **Synaptic plasticity**: a model of input-timing-dependent plasticity at cortico- and thalamo-striatal synapses, calibrated on ex vivo data, in collaboration with Laurent Venance's laboratory.
* **Astrocytes and neuronal synchronization**: how astrocytes modulate the spontaneous switching between Up and Down states in cortical networks ([PLOS Comput. Biol., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010296)).
* **Systems memory consolidation**: a coupled neural field model of the hippocampus and neocortex, showing how neocortical size and hippocampal neurogenesis can explain the standard consolidation theory ([J. Theor. Biol., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2024.111818)).
